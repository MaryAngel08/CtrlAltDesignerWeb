About Me

I'm Mary — a website designer who treats clarity as a design principle, not an afterthought. My process pairs a considered visual system with a real, functional build: no dead-end mockup that never leaves Figma, no template stretched past its limits.

Every project starts with a clear read on the brand and its audience, and ends with a site that reads just as well from a phone in a pocket as it does on a wide desktop.

I design and build in the same pass. Type, spacing, and interaction get decided together, then shipped as a working page in Cursor or Webflow — not a file that waits for someone else to interpret it.

The aim is always the same: a site that feels inevitable. Clear hierarchy, quiet motion, and a system you can still use two years from now without starting over.
