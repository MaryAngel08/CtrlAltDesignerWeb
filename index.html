About Me

About Me

I'm Mary, a website designer who builds with intention, where every choice earns its place. I turn a coherent visual language into a live, working site: no design sitting unfinished in a file, no theme forced beyond what it can hold.

Each brief begins with a close look at the brand and the people it serves, and finishes as a site that holds together just as cleanly from a small phone screen to a wide desktop display.

The goal is a site that feels settled and sure: strong hierarchy, restrained motion, and a system you can still run two years from now without a rebuild.
